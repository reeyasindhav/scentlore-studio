Problem Statement:

Fragrance enthusiasts and casual buyers often struggle to discover and evaluate perfumes online because scent is an invisible, sensory experience that standard e-commerce platforms fail to convey clearly. Most online stores rely on dense text descriptions and generic marketing, making it difficult for users to understand how complex fragrance notes evolve, determine which scents fit specific moods or occasions, or find reliable peer reviews. Additionally, collectors lack a dedicated, visually intuitive space to organise, track, and share their personal perfume wardrobes. Scentlore addresses this gap by creating an interactive discovery and review platform that translates abstract scent profiles into intuitive visual elements, offers targeted mood and occasion filtering, and provides seamless collection management for fragrance lovers.
